Walking tour — Colosseum to Trevi Fountain (~4–5 hours)

⚠ Print your Parco archeologico tickets before leaving home (booking #OCO3989330, PDF in your ticketcolosseo.com email). Bring a photo ID matching the booking name — entry denied without it.
1
Parco archeologico del Colosseo
Your tickets (booking #OCO3989330) include the park, Roman Forum, and Palatine Hill. Start here first while energy is highest. The Forum ruins alone take an hour to wander, and Palatine Hill has sweeping views over the entire complex.
Start point · Plan 1.5–2.5 hrs
2
Vittoriano / Piazza Venezia
Walk up Via dei Fori Imperiali (~12 min). The massive white marble monument nicknamed "the wedding cake." Free to enter — the rooftop terrace has sweeping 360° views and is often overlooked by tourists.
~12 min walk · 20–30 min visit
3
Pantheon
Walk north through the historic center (~20 min). Nearly 2,000-year-old unreinforced concrete dome. The oculus floods the interior with light. Entry requires a timed ticket (~€5) — book ahead or queue on arrival.
~20 min walk · 30–45 min visit
4
Trevi Fountain
7 min from the Pantheon. Rome's most famous fountain — 85 feet wide, carved from travertine marble. Toss a coin with your right hand over your left shoulder to guarantee your return. Very crowded midday — late afternoon light is more magical.
~7 min walk · Spend as long as you like
5
Piazza Navona (optional)
Beautiful baroque piazza built over a Roman stadium, with three fountains including Bernini's Fountain of the Four Rivers. Surrounded by cafés — a good rest stop before dinner in Trastevere.
~10 min from Trevi · 20–30 min visit

Naples afternoon — ~5 hours free after excursion returns (~1 PM)

★ The port is 10 minutes on foot to the city center — no taxi needed. Walk out of the terminal along the waterfront and you're already there.
🍕
Pizza — first priority, non-negotiable
Naples invented pizza. L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele (Via Cesare Sersale 1) — Margherita or Marinara only, ~€5–8, cash only. Expect a queue; get a number at the door. If the wait is too long, Sorbillo on Via dei Tribunali is the next best. Both are within a 15–20 min walk of the port. This is the meal of the day.
Via Cesare Sersale 1 (da Michele) or Via dei Tribunali 32 (Sorbillo) · 15–20 min walk
1
Castel Nuovo (Maschio Angioino)
The dramatic 13th-century fortress right at the port — you'll walk past it leaving the terminal. Impressive from outside at no cost, or enter the Civic Museum for €6 (Mon–Sat 8:30 AM–5:30 PM). Five towers and an ornate triumphal arch — very photogenic.
Right at the port · 30 min or just admire from outside
2
Piazza del Plebiscito & Royal Palace
Naples' largest square, flanked by the Basilica of San Francesco di Paola (free, Pantheon-inspired dome) and the Royal Palace (throne rooms and state apartments are genuinely spectacular). Even just the scale of the piazza is impressive.
~10 min walk from port · 20–30 min
3
Via Toledo & Toledo Metro Station
Naples' main shopping street. Duck into the Toledo Metro Station — the Daily Telegraph named it the most impressive metro station in the world. Buy a €1.10 ticket, ride down, and look up: thousands of tiny blue-and-white tiles replicating the ocean. Ride back up — you don't even need to take a train. Also here: Galleria Umberto I, a beautiful 19th-century glass-domed arcade.
~10 min walk from Piazza del Plebiscito · 15–20 min
4
Castel dell'Ovo & Lungomare waterfront
Naples' oldest fortress on a small peninsula — free to enter. Outstanding views of the Gulf of Naples and Mount Vesuvius. The Lungomare Caracciolo waterfront promenade stretches along the bay — perfect wind-down before heading back to the ship. Pick up a sfogliatella (shell-shaped Campanian pastry) from any café near the waterfront.
~15 min walk from Piazza del Plebiscito · Easy walk back to port
⚠ Keep bags close in Naples — standard big-city awareness for pickpocketing in tourist areas. Cross-body bag, phone in a pocket, valuables out of sight. The areas above are all fine during the day.

Getting from Civitavecchia to Lido di Ostia

ℹ No direct route — everything goes through Rome. The good news: the Civitavecchia train stops at Roma Ostiense, right next to the Porta San Paolo station for the Roma–Lido di Ostia line. A well-connected two-train journey without going all the way to Termini.
Civitavecchia cruise port → Lido di Ostia · ~2 hours total
Recommended
Two trains via Roma Ostiense
Step 1: Port shuttle to Civitavecchia station (~€6, 10–15 min).
Step 2: Trenitalia train Civitavecchia → Roma Ostiense (~55–70 min, ~€5–15).
Step 3: Walk ~2 min to Porta San Paolo station — take the Roma–Lido Metromare direct to Lido Centro (~31 min, ~€1.50). Short walk or taxi to hotel.
Total: ~€13–22 for both · ~2 hours

Phone & calling guide

T-Mobile international coverage

T-Mobile Magenta and higher plans include free data and texting in Italy, Greece, and Turkey (data at 2G speeds — good for maps and messages). Calls cost $0.25/min, or use Wi-Fi Calling for free. Confirm your specific plan at T-Mobile.com before leaving.

On the cruise ship: Turn off cellular roaming entirely and use ship Wi-Fi for everything. Ship roaming charges are extremely high.

Offline maps: Download Google Maps offline regions for Rome, Santorini, Mykonos, Kusadasi/Ephesus, and Naples before leaving home. Works with no signal.

VAT reclaim guide

What is VAT?

VAT is Europe's sales tax (~22% in Italy, ~24% in Greece). As US citizens, you're eligible to reclaim it on qualifying purchases over €70 at participating stores — look for a "Tax Free Shopping" sign.

At the store

Ask the cashier: "Can I get a VAT refund form?" They'll fill out a Global Blue or Planet form and attach your receipt. Keep all of these safe throughout the trip.

At FCO on June 15 — step by step

Step 1. Before checking your bags, find the Customs / Tax Refund desk in the FCO departures hall. Show purchases, receipts, and completed VAT forms — the officer stamps the forms.

Step 2. Take the stamped forms to the Global Blue or Planet counter (also in departures) for your refund as cash euros or card reimbursement.

Allow 30–45 extra minutes at the airport for this process.

Onboard ship purchases

Guest Services will provide VAT claim forms onboard. Keep all onboard retail and spa receipts throughout the cruise.

Nothing to prepare now — just keep receipts as you go.
